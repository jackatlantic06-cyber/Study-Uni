# Study-Uni brand and content rules

## What Study-Uni is
- study-uni.ie: exam revision for University College Cork (UCC) students.
- Past exam papers, marking schemes and MCQ practice banks for 100+ UCC courses/modules, all in one place.
- Signing up is free with a UCC student email (@umail.ucc.ie). There is a paid Pro membership that unlocks more (e.g. marking schemes and quizzes). Never state prices, discounts or "free forever" — say "free to sign up".
- It is NOT run by UCC and is not official. Never imply UCC endorses it. Never use the UCC crest or logo.

## The running angle
"All UCC exam papers in one place." Finding UCC past papers is a known pain (scattered across library pages, Canvas, group chats). Lean into that relief.

## Voice
- An Irish UCC student talking to other students. Short, funny, relatable, a bit self-deprecating. Cork/Irish phrasing is welcome in moderation ("grand", "class", "the library at 2am", "Boole", "the Glucksman", "Mardyke"), never forced or stage-Irish.
- Plain words, short sentences, no corporate tone, no "Unlock your potential", no "game-changer", no em-dash-heavy copywriting.
- Emojis: a few, not every line.

## Hard rules (never break)
1. Frame everything as revision and past-paper PRACTICE. Never say or imply "exam answers", "get the answers", "answer key", "cheat", "leaked", "upcoming exam questions", "guaranteed pass/grade", "pass without studying", "hack/beat the exam", or anything that suggests academic dishonesty or contract cheating. Our ad account was already limited for this.
2. Marking schemes are "official marking schemes from past years to check your practice answers against" — never "the answers".
3. No false or unverifiable claims. Don't invent statistics, student numbers, testimonials or grades.
4. Don't name or mock real lecturers, students, modules' staff or competitors.
5. Nothing about other universities unless the brief says so.
6. Faceless content: no people on camera, no "I'm Jack" — the account speaks as Study-Uni.
7. Respect mental health: exam stress is fine to joke about, never self-harm, drinking-to-cope or all-nighter glorification.
